Chat Support Practice Samples

Order tracking enquiry

Customer: “My order has not arrived yet. Can you help?”

Agent: “I’m sorry your order hasn’t arrived yet. I understand your concern. Could you please share your order number? I’ll check the tracking information and update you.”

Wrong product received

Customer: “I ordered a blue shirt, but received a black one.”

Agent: “I’m sorry you received the wrong item. I understand how frustrating that can be. Could you please confirm your order number so I can review the order details and explain the next steps?”

Refund enquiry

Customer: “I want a refund for my order.”

Agent: “I can help you with your refund enquiry. Could you please share your order number? I’ll review the order details and the applicable refund policy before advising you on the next steps.”

Support Workflow Knowledge

Ticket: A record of a customer’s request or issue.

Public reply: A message visible to the customer.

Internal note: A note for the support team, not visible to the customer.

Priority: The urgency assigned to a ticket.

Status: The current state of a ticket, such as Open, Pending, or Solved.

Professional Principles

  • Be polite, patient, and clear.
  • Verify order details before making promises.
  • Never request passwords, one-time codes, or full payment-card details.
  • Follow the company’s refund, return, and privacy policies.
  • Do not claim work experience or software expertise you have not yet gained.
